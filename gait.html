Gait Assessment

Position the camera to the side, perpendicular to a walking path with at least 4-5 steps of space. Stand back so your full body stays in frame as you walk across it — one or two passes is plenty.

This is a movement-screening tool, not a medical diagnosis. Only your near-side leg (facing the camera) is tracked reliably from a single camera.

This on-screen report shows kinematics (timing, joint angles) only. A separate local desktop tool can produce a fuller clinical report from this same recording — it runs on a computer, not through this website, and no video is uploaded anywhere by either tool.

Gait Assessment Report

This screen reports movement measurements captured by on-device computer vision from a single side-view camera. It does not measure step width, inter-limb symmetry, or joint forces during gait, and is not a medical diagnosis. Share this report with a physical therapist or physician for clinical interpretation.
